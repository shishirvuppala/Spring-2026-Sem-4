# Motivation and Review

I talked with a few seniors and they said this was a really good course on ML and recommended it to me, and I wanted to take atleast 1 CS elective this semester and the only other option was CS766 which i wasnt as interested in so i decided to take this.

THe course itself was really nice, the topics covered were quite expansive and the assignments did a very good job of giving us the feel of implementing the algorithms. the professor and Tas are also quite chill with deadlines and vivas. I liked this course more than the core AIML course we had in 4th sem. 

The Midsem and Endsem were pretty difficult with really low means and medians(mean and median were below 25%). assignments were easy to score in, for the project you had to pick a recent medical image computing related paper and implement whatever the papaer talks about and present it to the professor. the assignments and project were done in groups of two.

Overall really enjoyed the course even though I did mess up in the midsem and endsem lol.
