## Course Logistics

DE 250 is a compulsory laboratory for all second year UG students

## Percent Distribution

**Weekly Videos & Quizzes** - 5%  

**Attendance** - 10%   
**Individual work per lab** - 10%  

**Quizzes** - 30% (3 quizzes of 10% each)  
**Weekly work per lab** - 30%  

**Exhibition** - 15%  


## Project Themes
There are six project themes for each section.

A. Unfolding Concepts of Geometry

B. Science of Extraction and Filtration

C. Illuminating Principles of Light

D. Comprehending Concepts of Calculus

E. Demystifying Thermodynamics

F. Physics of Musical Instruments


## Course Description

The point of this course is to ideate a problem statement related to your theme in the first half of the course and then in the second half, ideate a solution/product/experiment for that problem statement you defined in the first half and then showcase it in an exhibition. The point of the weekly labs is to introduce ideation tools like brainstorming or action analysis etc. to give a methodical approach to design. 

So every week they introduce some kind of design thinking skill, (in the weekly videos you're supposed to watch) and then in the lab you have to apply that to your theme/product to improve it or help in ideation. then for some labs you need to write everything you ideated onto a A1 sheet of paper and then submit it.

The weekly labs can feel like a chore at times where you're doing a bunch of pointless stuff for no reason, However that may just be my experience and there are people who enjoyed the weekly labs(even I enjoyed a lot of labs but some of them were really annoying to me). 

Now in both half semesters you had to create an experiment/product to understand the problem more deeply or to solve the problem statement, I had the theme of thermodynamics and we were kinda uncreative so both half sems we just tried to make a heat engine. the key word here is tried because both times it didnt really work that well.(In fact in the exhibition our model didnt work and so we had to use motors and other components to simulate what it would look like if it did work lol, the examiner did grill us very hard about this tho :( ). So, because our models never really worked I didnt really like this course, but a team who actually did creative ideation and got a idea which could work and implemented it properly would probably have more fun than us.

All in all, the idea of this course is pretty good to teach creative thinking to engineers, but because of all the BT we had from our topic, (I would have preferred any other topic than thermo) I didnt enjoy this course and wouldn't really want to do something like this again.

## Important Points

- You can miss 2 weeks worth of embedded weekly quizzes in our year and still get full marks but I dont know if thats true for every year or not
- If your TA likes you or knows that you have actually done work then your individual work per lab marks will be good
- Be very careful in the quizzes, the quizzes themselves are easy only but dont underestimate them and make sure to study properly. silly mistakes can cost you a lot
- Weekly work per lab is basically free marks as long as you produce somewhat decent output (doesnt have to be the greatest output ever for good marks basically)
- EXhibition is very rng so cant do much but they do some weird normalizaiton so that may help you out

TO get a good grade in this course
- Be friends with your TA or at least dont piss them off so they give good marks
- DO NOT mess up the quizzes try to get 29 or 30 as total otherwise it might be a grade drop. if you have 30 then you mostly likely will get an AA.
- obviously dont miss any labs and if you do miss make sure to have a very good reason. missing one lab is losing 5% which is insanely high for this course.

**1 percent can lead to a drop in ~100-150 ranks and so do be careful**
